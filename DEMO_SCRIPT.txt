SCENE 1 — THE PROBLEM (20 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Open https://neobustest.vercel.app/ in Chrome, full screen.

ACTION: Let the page load. Slowly scroll down. Click on route selection
dropdown — show it doesnt work. Click the search button — show error popup.

VOICEOVER:

"Imagine you are an investor or a product manager looking at this bus booking
website. At first glance, it looks fine — clean layout, branding is there,
the navigation works."

"But what if I told you this site has critical bugs that would prevent a
single ticket from being booked?"

"The route selector doesnt load. The date picker is broken. And the moment
you try to search for a bus, you hit an error."

"These arent cosmetic issues — they are revenue-killing bugs. But heres the
thing — a manual QA team would take hours to find all of this. What if we
could do it in 3 minutes, with AI?"


SCENE 2 — INTRODUCING SENTRA (15 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Open http://localhost:5173 — Sentra homepage.

ACTION: Show the full homepage. Hover over the "6 Active Agents" badge.
Point to the agent names listed on the homepage.

VOICEOVER:

"This is Sentra — an AI-powered website reliability engine."

"Sentra deploys 6 specialized AI agents, each with a unique mission —
Flow testing, Feature testing, UI/UX validation, Exploratory crawling,
Functional verification, and Console error monitoring."

"They all work in parallel, in separate browser tabs, just like a full
QA team — but powered by AI."


SCENE 3 — WHAT MAKES SENTRA DIFFERENT (10 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Still on Sentra homepage.

VOICEOVER:

"What makes Sentra different from traditional testing tools is that it
doesnt need test scripts. No Selenium. No Cypress configs. No setup."

"You just give it a URL, describe what to test in plain English, and
optionally upload a PRD — a product requirements document — and Sentras
AI figures out the rest."


SCENE 4 — CONFIGURING THE TEST (20 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Click "Analyse" or navigate to the testing interface.

ACTION:
  1. Type https://neobustest.vercel.app/ in the URL field
  2. In the Text field, type:
     "Bus booking site. Test the full booking flow — route selection,
      date picker, search, seat selection, and payment."
  3. (Optional) Upload or paste a PRD document
  4. Click Start Test

VOICEOVER:

"I will paste the URL of that bus booking site we just saw."

"In the description, I will tell Sentra what to focus on — the booking
flow, route selection, search, seat selection, and payment."

"You can also upload a PRD if you have one — Sentra will use it as
context for smarter, more targeted testing."

(beat)

"Now I will hit Start Test — and this is where the magic happens."


SCENE 5 — LIVE AGENT EXECUTION (45-60 seconds, SPEED UP TO 3x)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Agents Panel opens. Show full dashboard with all 6 agent cards,
risk score meter, and live issue feed.

NOTE: Record this at normal speed, then speed up to 3x in editing.
Real-time duration is about 2-3 minutes, shown as 45-60 seconds.

VOICEOVER (narrate over the sped-up footage):

"You are now watching all 6 AI agents work simultaneously. Let me walk
you through what is happening."

"Agent A — the Planner — just launched the browser and navigated to the
site. It is doing reconnaissance — identifying the site type, extracting
the page structure, figuring out which flows to test."

(pause as Agent B activates)

"Agent B — the Dispatcher — is now distributing tasks to all 6 testing
agents based on what Agent A discovered."

(pause as Agents C through H light up one by one)

"Now all 6 testing agents are running in parallel."

"Agent C — the Flow Agent — is walking through the main user journey.
Search for a bus, browse results, select a seat, proceed to checkout."

"Agent D — the Feature Agent — is testing specific features like seat
selection, price calculations, and form validation."

"Agent E — the UI/UX Agent — is checking layout consistency, images,
banners, and responsive design."

"Agent F — the Exploratory Agent — is crawling every link on the site,
looking for 404 errors and broken pages."

"Agent G — the Functional Agent — is verifying prices match between
pages, descriptions are correct, and currency formatting is consistent."

"Agent H — the Console Agent — is monitoring the browsers developer
console for JavaScript errors, failed API calls, and network failures."

(point to the risk score updating on screen)

"Watch the Risk Score on the left — it is climbing in real-time as bugs
are discovered. You can see the Live Issues feed categorizing them by
severity — Critical, High, Medium."

(point to agent cards showing Complete or Done with Issues)

"Agents are finishing one by one. Some found issues, some passed cleanly.
The entire test — 6 agents, dozens of actions — completed in under
3 minutes."


SCENE 6 — THE REPORT (15 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Click the "Report" tab at the top.

ACTION: Point to the reliability score circle. Point to the stats bar.
Point to the AI-generated summary.

VOICEOVER:

"Now lets look at the results. Sentra generates a full reliability report."

"The Reliability Score is 35 out of 100 — thats a Critical Risk rating.
This site is not production-ready."

"All 6 agents completed their tasks. They performed a total of multiple
actions across every page, with clear pass and fail counts."

"The AI has written a natural language summary explaining exactly what
it found — route selection is broken, error popups prevent any booking,
and core features are completely non-functional."


SCENE 7 — ISSUES BREAKDOWN (20 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Scroll down to the Issues section in the report.

ACTION: Show the list of issues. Click on or highlight a specific
critical issue to show its details.

VOICEOVER:

"Here is the detailed issues breakdown. Each bug is documented with:"

"The severity — Critical, High, or Medium."

"What was expected versus what actually happened."

"The business impact — why this matters to the product and to revenue."

"And the exact location — which page and which element is affected."

(highlight a specific critical issue)

"For example, this Critical issue — Route selection dropdown is
non-functional — was found by Agent D on the homepage."

"Expected behavior was a working dropdown with bus routes."

"Actual behavior — completely unresponsive."

"Business impact — no customer can book a single ticket."


SCENE 8 — EVIDENCE SCREENSHOTS (15 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Scroll to the Evidence Screenshots section.

ACTION: Show the grid of screenshots with agent labels underneath each.

VOICEOVER:

"Sentra doesnt just report bugs — it proves them."

"Every bug comes with an evidence screenshot captured at the exact moment

"You can see which agent captured each screenshot and what bug it
corresponds to."

"This is the kind of evidence you need for developer handoffs, sprint
planning, and stakeholder reviews."


SCENE 9 — RECOMMENDATIONS AND STRENGTHS (15 seconds)
================================================================================

SCREEN: Scroll to the Strengths and Recommendations sections.

VOICEOVER:

"Sentra also identifies what is working well — in this case, the site

"And most importantly, it gives prioritized recommendations."

"Fix the route selection first. Then the search functionality. Then the
